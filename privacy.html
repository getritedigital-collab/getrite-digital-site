Privacy Policy — Getrite Digital

Effective date: November 12, 2026 • Contact: Getritedigital@gmail.com

Getrite Digital provides social media content, video, and branding services to small businesses. This policy explains what information we collect through getritedigital.com, our intake form, and email, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or services, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact and business details: name, business name, email address, and other contact details you give us on the intake form, by email, or in messages.
  • Service information: information about your business, services, and goals that you share so we can create content and advertising for you.
  • Files and content you provide: photos, logos, text, music, video, and other brand assets you provide for us to use in your content.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number on our website. Stripe may collect payment and billing information under its own privacy policy.
  • Website information: basic information about how our website is used, such as pages visited and device or browser type, collected through our hosting and website tools.
  • Advertising information: if we run ads for you, information connected to those ads through Meta (Facebook and Instagram), such as ad performance and leads who contact you through an ad.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to provide our services — create, review, schedule, and post content, videos, and branding you have ordered; communicate with you about your plan, content, approvals, and support questions; take payment and manage your monthly plan — Basic, Standard, or Premium — billed through Stripe; set up and manage advertising you have agreed to, where ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta and is not included in our service price unless we agree otherwise in writing; improve our website and services, and keep records we need for our business and legal obligations.

We do not use the photos, logos, or other assets you provide for any client other than you, unless you give us permission.

3. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run our services, with providers such as Stripe (processing payments), Meta (Facebook / Instagram — posting content and, if agreed, running ads), Google (intake form, email, documents, and spreadsheets), Cloudflare (hosting and protecting our website), and Zapier (moving form and payment information between the tools above).

Each provider handles information under its own privacy policy. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our clients, or the safety of others.

Content you approve for posting — for example, a social media post or promotional video — will be public once it is posted, as you instructed.

4. Cookies and tracking

Our website and the providers listed above may use cookies and similar tools to make the website work, remember basic settings, and understand how visitors use the site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the website may not work as well. If we add analytics or advertising tools that use cookies in a new way, we will update this policy first.

5. How long we keep information and how we protect it

We keep client information for as long as you are a client and for a reasonable time afterwards, so we can keep business, payment, and tax records and respond to questions or disputes. We take reasonable steps to protect information, such as using established providers for payments, hosting, and email. No website or email system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please do not send passwords, full card numbers, or other highly sensitive information through our intake form or by email.

6. Your choices and rights

You can email us at Getritedigital@gmail.com to ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date, ask us to delete your personal information where we are not required to keep it for payment, tax, or legal records, or stop marketing emails. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to check who you are before we change or delete information.

7. Children's privacy

Our website and services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will be posted on this page with a new effective date at the top. Continuing to use our website or services after a change means you accept the updated policy.
